### en-US (`release-notes/en-US.txt`)
New
• Get directions: from a box's page, Maps opens with the walking route.
• Show on map: the map centers on the box in one tap.
• Handier preview: tap anywhere to open the box page, bigger photo, and a badge shows the box condition (good, fair, poor).
• No photo yet? A camera invites you to add the first one.
• Long-press the GPS coordinates to copy them.

Improvements
• Settings refresh on their own: the status of your submissions shows up without pulling down.
• Tapping the Settings tab again takes you back to its top, like Map and List.

Fixes
• Nearby box alert: no more notifications while driving (above 7 km/h).

Thank you to everyone who adds boxes, shares photos and reports changes: you are the ones keeping the map alive and accurate.
