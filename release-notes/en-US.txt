Version 1.3.0 (build 16)

Required update: if your version is too old, a screen asks you to update the app before continuing.

Box location: you can confirm a box is really there, or suggest its exact position on the map, when you are close enough. Suggested positions are checked before being published.

Nearby box alert (off by default, Settings › Location): a reminder when you pass within 50 m of a box. Your location stays on your phone. An alert you have already seen won't come back for 30 days.

Removal: the report button only works within 100 m of the box.

Photos: a new box requires a photo taken on site. No people should appear in the photo.

Fixes: photo preview and carousel, "My submissions" and "My removal requests", build number shown in settings.

Thank you to everyone who adds boxes, shares photos and reports changes: you are the ones keeping the map alive and accurate.

Special thanks to Loane Morandeau, who reported a security issue that is fixed in this version.
